About Us

The story behind South Sudan's trusted printing and branding partner

Our Story

Founded in 2023, Durablelink Prints and Brands was born from a vision to bring world-class printing and branding services to South Sudan. Based in Gudele One, Juba, we set out to fill a gap in the market for high-quality, reliable, and affordable printing solutions.

As a proudly South Sudanese-owned company, we understand the unique needs of local businesses, NGOs, government agencies, and entrepreneurs. From business cards to full vehicle wraps, we provide end-to-end branding solutions that help organizations make a lasting impression.

Today, with hundreds of satisfied clients and a growing portfolio of successful projects, Durablelink continues to push the boundaries of what's possible in printing and branding across the region.
